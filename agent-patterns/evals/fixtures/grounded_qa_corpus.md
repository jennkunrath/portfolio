# Acme Corp Employee Leave & Remote Work Policy (2026)

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time salaried employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year. Accrual occurs at a rate of 1.66 days per month. Unused PTO up to a maximum of 5 days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 8 dedicated sick days annually, available starting on day one of employment. Sick leave does not carry over into subsequent years and is not compensable upon termination.

## 3. Remote Work & Core Hours
Employees are permitted to work remotely on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Time. All team members are expected to be available on Slack and email during core hours regardless of physical work location.
