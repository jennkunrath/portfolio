## What it's for

Per the suite's own description: "Isolate internal file search friction vectors. Models the direct economic impact of transitioning unstructured repositories into structured, source-cited contextual semantic discovery endpoints." Translated out of the product copy: this is the calculator for a buyer whose actual bottleneck is that their people can't find things — unstructured file repositories, no source citation, no semantic search — and who wants the dollar value of fixing just that, independent of whether an agent is ever built on top of it.

## How this relates to the other solution calculators

[Custom AI Agent Model](custom-ai-agent-model.md) covers the agent half of a deployment; this covers the retrieval half. [Combined Solution ROI](combined-solution-roi.md) is what a buyer uses once they've decided they need both and wants the combined, non-overlapping figure. This calculator is the one to reach for when the RAG layer is the whole ask — a knowledge-base or internal-search modernization project with no agent attached.

## Likely structure, based on the shared architecture

Following the pattern shared across the suite, this calculator most likely models value drivers around search-time reduction (labor hours lost to manual file hunting), answer-accuracy or rework avoided (cost of acting on stale or wrong information found the old way), and some measure of repository scale or adoption.
